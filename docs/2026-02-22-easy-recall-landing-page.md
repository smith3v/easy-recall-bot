# Easy Recall Bot

## Remember vocabulary with short, smart Telegram practice.

Easy Recall Bot helps you build lasting recall with short sessions in Telegram.
Practice what matters, at the right moment, and keep your progress steady
without overloading your day.
Each card has its own learning progress, so words come back smartly when they
need reinforcement and stay in memory longer.

## Why learners choose Easy Recall Bot

### Smart timing

![Smart timing icon](img/Icon smart timing.png)

Review words when they are most useful for memory, so practice feels focused
and efficient.
The bot tracks progress per card and adapts review timing to what you know
well and what still needs work.

### Better retention

![Memory retention icon](img/Icon memory retention.png)

Use active recall in small steps that help move vocabulary from short-term
memory to long-term recall.

### Gentle reminders

![Gentle reminders icon](img/Icon gentle reminders.png)

Choose reminder times that fit your routine and stay consistent with less
stress.

## How it works

1. Start with `/start` and choose your language direction.
2. Practice daily in short sessions with `/review` or quick check-ins with
`/getpair`.
3. Strengthen recall over time, with optional quiz sessions in `/game`.

## What you can do

- Start with built-in onboarding vocabulary.
- Import and export your vocabulary anytime.
- Track progress for every card with smart resurfacing over time.
- Adjust cards per session and reminder schedule in `/settings`.
- Share suggestions directly with `/feedback`.
